Scanny 2.0 is a rewrite of the app in Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform: one codebase for Android and iOS, in 12 languages.

## Scan

- Scans with the camera, one code or several at once, or from an image of the gallery.
- The zoom shown while scanning, back to 1× in a touch; on an iPhone, the macro lens up close.
- Supported formats: QR Code, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, Code 39, Code 93, Code 128, ITF and Codabar.
- Every content read and shown field by field, with its action:
  - links and deep links of apps;
  - e-mail, SMS, MMS, phone call;
  - Wi-Fi networks, WPA3 and enterprise ones included, their password hidden until touched;
  - contacts (vCard and MeCard) and calendar events;
  - places on the map;
  - one-time passwords, added to an authenticator;
  - bitcoin payments.
- History of the scans, groups of a multiple scan included, kept as long as chosen, each code with its format.
- Favorites, put from the detail of a code, right after a scan or by holding a row of the history: gathered at the top of the history and never purged.

## Create

- QR codes, Data Matrix, Aztec codes, PDF417 and barcodes, with every field of each format, required fields marked.
- QR codes customized: colors, gradient, shapes of the dots and corners, logo, error correction, with a readability warning.
- A scanned or created code edited into a new one; codes shared or saved as images, in PNG or in SVG, sharp at any size.

## Languages

- English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

## Under the hood

- Corrected Data Matrix and Aztec encoders, checked against ZXing.
- Baseline profiles for a faster start.
- Crash reports through Crashlytics, on Android and iOS.
- A splash screen.
- CI on every pull request, and publishing in one click.
